About Me

Hi, I'm Alex! I'm a web design student and barista with a love for creative, slightly chaotic aesthetics.

I'm currently building my skills in front-end development, focusing on creating responsive, accessible websites using HTML and CSS. I enjoy fun color palettes in most of my designs.

When I'm not coding or making coffee, I'm usually drawing, designing stickers, or collecting inspiration from things I love. I'm especially interested in combining playful design with practical web experiences.

My goal is to grow into a web designer who can create work that feels both professional and uniquely expressive.

Design style: grounded, colorful, and a little unconventional.
