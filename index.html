Welcome

Hi, I’m Alex! I'm a web design student and barista building my skills in front-end development.

I focus on creating clean, responsive websites using HTML and CSS, with an emphasis on structure, accessibility, and visual balance. This portfolio showcases my work as I grow in web development, from layout experiments to fully responsive multi-page sites.

I’m interested in combining solid technical foundations with creative design choices that give each project its own personality. Outside of coding, I draw inspiration from everyday life and things I enjoy - frogs, cats, and playful visual styles - which often influences my design decisions and color choices.

Explore my projects to see what I’ve been building and how my skills are developing over time.
